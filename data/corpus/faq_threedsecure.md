# FAQ: 3-D Secure

**Q: When is 3DS required?**
A: Where regulation mandates SCA for the transaction.

**Q: Why did the challenge time out?**
A: A slow or unreachable ACS; see PAY-5005 and PAY-6006.

**Q: Are subscriptions challenged?**
A: Usually not — merchant-initiated transactions often qualify for an exemption.
