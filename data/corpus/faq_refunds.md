# FAQ: Refunds

**Q: How long does a refund take?**
A: 1-5 business days depending on the issuer.

**Q: Can I refund more than was captured?**
A: No — that fails with `refund_exceeds_captured`.

**Q: Can I refund an authorized-only payment?**
A: No. Void it instead; refunds require a captured payment.
